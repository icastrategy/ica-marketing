Data collected
When you fill in the contact form, book a call or write to me: your name, your email address and the information you choose to share about your project (and your phone number if you provide it).
Why
Only to answer your request, prepare your quote and follow up on our collaboration if we work together. Your data is never sold, never used for advertising and never added to a newsletter without your consent.

Your contact or quote request, then the contract if we work together.
Retention
Three years after our last exchange if we do not work together. For clients, the duration of the collaboration, then accounting records for ten years, as required by law.

Your rights
You can ask to access, correct, delete or retrieve your data, or object to its use, by writing to ica.strategy@gmail.com. I reply within one month. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
