about me

I'm Inesse. What I love most about my work is telling visual stories that truly resonate with people.

I built my expertise in advertising (JCDecaux), tech (Invent App), and events, learning to handle words, visuals, and strategy with as much rigor as passion.

What drives me? Taking a raw project and giving it a real identity: something that's seen, that's felt, that lasts.

I'm someone who observes, who listens, and who always seeks to understand what's behind a brand before writing a single word or creating a single image.

To me, marketing is above all a human question.

I'm convinced that good marketing doesn't settle for being visible. It creates connection, tells a story, drives action.

And because good ideas deserve to travel, I work in French, English, Portuguese, and Spanish.
