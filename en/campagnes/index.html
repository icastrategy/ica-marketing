Frequently asked questions

Before you launch.

01What do I need to provide to get started?

Your goals, some information about your customers, your visuals if you have any, and access to your ad accounts. If you don't have them yet, I'll help you set them up.

02Is the ad budget included in the quote?

No. The quote covers my work: strategy, ad creation, monitoring and reporting. The ad budget is paid directly to the platforms (Meta, Google), and we set it together based on your goals.

03When will I see the first results?

The first meaningful results come after 7 to 14 days: that is how long the platforms need to learn how to find the right people. Campaigns then keep improving week after week.

04Which platforms?

Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads. I recommend the ones where your customers really are, not necessarily all of them.
