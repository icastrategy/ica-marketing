FAQ
01What are your timelines?

It depends on the scope of the project. The exact timeline is stated in your quote. If you have a deadline, tell me in our first conversation and I'll tell you honestly whether it's doable.

02How does a quote work?

Book a free 30-minute discovery call, or describe your project in the form. I'll then send you a free, personalized quote within 24 hours. Work starts as soon as you approve it.

03Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm based in Paris and work with clients across France and abroad. We talk by video call, email or phone, whichever suits you.

04Which languages do you work in?

French, English, Portuguese and Spanish. I can also adapt your content from one language to another.

05What happens after delivery?

You receive all source files, in the right formats for each medium. Two rounds of revisions are included. After delivery, I remain available to answer your questions and make small adjustments.
