What I do

My expertise

Strategy
Strategy and editorial line
Before posting, we know why and for whom. I decide where to be present, what to say and how often.
  • Audit: what already works on your accounts, and what holds them back.
  • Platforms: the ones where your clients are, not necessarily all of them.
  • Editorial line: your topics, your tone and your formats.
Content
Content creation
Posts that make people stop, read and get in touch.
  • Editorial calendar: your posts for the month, planned in advance.
  • Copywriting: captions, carousels and LinkedIn posts in your tone.

Tracking and adjustments
A strategy needs steering. Every month, we look at what works and adjust.

FAQ

Before posting.

01Do you post directly on my accounts?

I can prepare the content for you to post, or schedule it directly for you. We choose the option that suits you.

02Which networks do you work on?

Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook. I recommend the ones where your clients really are, not necessarily all of them.

03How many posts per week?

Posting regularly matters more than posting a lot: two to three posts a week are often enough. We set the pace based on your goals and budget.

04When will I see results?

Social media takes consistency: allow two to three months to see real growth in your visibility and enquiries.
